Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Dailimit, an Android budgeting application developed in Finland, handles information.

App data

Dailimit does not require an account. Budgets, expenses (including optional names and dates), budgeting periods and preferences are processed and stored locally on your device to provide the app's functionality. The app does not send these data to the developer, and the developer cannot access them. Dailimit does not include advertising, analytics or tracking services and does not sell budgeting data.

Backup and restoration

Android may back up, transfer and restore app data, including budgeting information, depending on your device and system settings. These services are provided by Android or your backup service provider, and are subject to their own privacy policies and settings.

Storage, security and deletion

Dailimit uses Android's private application storage. The developer does not operate a server for your budgeting data. You can delete individual expenses in the app. To remove all locally stored Dailimit data, use Android's app storage settings to clear the app's data. System-managed backup copies are controlled separately through your device or backup service settings.

Support and privacy inquiries

If you contact the developer, your email address and the information you choose to provide are processed to respond to your inquiry, based on the legitimate interest in providing support. This information is handled through the developer's email service (Gmail) and retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the inquiry or meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid including sensitive financial information in support messages.

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal data held by the developer, restriction of processing, or object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

This website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. This page does not include analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about its practices.

Contact

For support or privacy-related questions, contact the developer of Dailimit at dailimit.app@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app or its data handling practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent update.
